Create these files:

1. settings.gradle.kts
2. build.gradle.kts (root)
3. app/build.gradle.kts
4. gradle.properties
5. gradle/wrapper/gradle-wrapper.properties
6. local.properties
7. app/proguard-rules.pro

And empty source directories with .gitkeep files.
